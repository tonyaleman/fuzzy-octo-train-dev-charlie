📚 How to Play

🎯 The Goal

Click each professor before they disappear. Clear five increasingly difficult levels to graduate.

🔥 Combos

Consecutive hits build your combo. At 3, 6, 10, and 15 consecutive hits your multiplier increases. Missing a professor resets it.

🎁 Special Targets

Gold targets can restore a life, slow the game, or award bonus points. Red 💀 targets are traps: clicking one costs a life.

🎓 Levels

Freshman → Sophomore → Junior → Senior → Finals. Targets become faster and smaller as you advance. Each level requires 10 successful professor hits.

⚡ Modes

Normal is balanced. Hard starts faster. Chaos is the fastest mode and produces more traps.
